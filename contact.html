Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

From bridal bookings and trials to travel, preparation and policies, here are answers to the most common questions.

Bridal Bookings

How far in advance should I book my bridal hair and makeup in Edmonton?

Bridal dates can fill quickly, especially during peak wedding seasons. I recommend reaching out 6–12 months in advance whenever possible. I intentionally take a limited number of bridal bookings so every bride receives the time, attention and experience she deserves.

How do I secure my wedding date?

A 40% deposit is required to officially secure your date. Your booking is not confirmed until the deposit has been received.

How can I inquire or book bridal hair and makeup?

Simply complete the Booking Form with your event details. You can also reach out through Instagram or WhatsApp if you have a question before submitting your inquiry.

Hair, Makeup & The Bridal Experience

What is your bridal makeup style?

I don’t believe in a one-style-fits-all approach. Every face is unique — different features, face shapes, skin types and personal preferences deserve a look created specifically for them.

My approach is always to enhance rather than transform, keeping the skin looking as natural and skin-like as possible while thoughtfully defining your features. Whether the final look is soft and minimal or a little more glamorous, the goal is always the same: for you to still look and feel like yourself — just elevated.

Do you provide bridal hairstyling as well?

Yes. Bridal bookings can include both professional makeup and hairstyling, along with the finishing details needed to complete your bridal look.

Do you offer bridal makeup and hair trials?

Yes. Bridal trials are available once you have your final wedding outfit and jewellery selected. Having these details finalized allows us to properly envision your complete bridal look and create hair and makeup that complements your outfit, jewellery, features and overall vision.

The goal of the trial is to bring all the elements together so you can see how your bridal look will feel as a whole before the wedding day.

How long should I allow for bridal hair and makeup?

I recommend approximately 3–3.5 hours for the complete bridal experience. This allows us to work without rushing and gives enough time for hair, makeup and final bridal detailing.

Can you help me decide what bridal makeup or hairstyle will suit me?

Absolutely. You don’t need to have your entire look figured out before your appointment. We can discuss your outfit, jewellery, face shape, hair, skin, inspiration and personal style to create a cohesive look that still feels like you.

Can you accommodate mothers, bridesmaids or other family members?

Yes. Hair and makeup can also be arranged for mothers, bridesmaids and other members of your party. Depending on the size and timing of your booking, a trusted team artist may assist so everyone is ready on time.

Travel & Destination Weddings

Do you offer on-location bridal hair and makeup in Edmonton?

Yes. I offer on-location bridal hair and makeup services in Edmonton and surrounding areas, as well as destination bookings. Travel fees may apply depending on the location and booking requirements.

Do you offer bridal hair and makeup in Punjab, India?

Yes. I take seasonal bridal hair and makeup bookings across Punjab, India, generally during the fall and winter wedding season. Availability is limited, so early inquiries are recommended.

Are you available as a destination bridal hair and makeup artist?

Yes. Destination weddings are welcome within Canada and internationally. A customized travel quote will be provided based on your wedding location, schedule and travel requirements.

Products, Skin & Preparation

What makeup products do you use for brides?

My professional kit includes a carefully selected mix of luxury and professional makeup brands chosen for their performance, longevity and finish. My kit is continuously updated so I can select products based on your individual skin and the look we are creating.

Do you provide bridal makeup for all skin tones?

Yes. I work with a wide range of skin tones and undertones and customize every complexion rather than using a one-look-fits-all approach.

What if I have sensitive or acne-prone skin?

Please let me know about any known sensitivities, allergies or skin concerns before your appointment. I’ll take them into consideration when selecting and applying products. If you have a known product or ingredient allergy, please disclose it before your service.

How should I prepare my skin and hair before my appointment?

You’ll receive preparation guidance before your appointment. In general, arriving with clean, well-prepped skin and clean, completely dry hair helps us achieve the best results.

Other Services

Do you offer non-bridal hair and makeup in Edmonton?

Yes. Non-bridal hair and makeup services are available for selected special occasions, pre-wedding events, photoshoots and other celebrations, subject to availability and booking requirements.

Do you offer professional makeup and hairstyling courses?

Yes. Education is an important part of Sanpreet Makeupstories. I offer professional makeup and hair training, self-makeup lessons and selected masterclasses. Current education opportunities can be found through the Education section or by submitting an inquiry.

Policies

What happens if I need to cancel or reschedule?

Your 40% deposit is non-refundable. With at least 30 days’ notice, it may be transferred to a new available date. Changes requested with less than 30 days’ notice are subject to the cancellation and rescheduling terms outlined in the Booking Policies.

What happens if I’m running late?

Please let me know as soon as possible if you are running behind. Arriving more than 15 minutes late without notice may affect the service timeline and may be subject to a late fee.
