Hi, I’m Sanpreet.

I’m a professional hair and makeup artist and educator based in Edmonton, Canada, and originally from Punjab, India.

Sanpreet Makeupstories began during a completely new chapter of my life. After becoming a mother, I made the decision to step away from my previous career and pursue something I genuinely loved — beauty, creativity and working closely with people.

What began as a leap into the unknown quickly became something much bigger. From building my bridal clientele and working across Canada and India to stepping into education, every stage has pushed me to grow as both an artist and a business owner.

Today, Sanpreet Makeupstories represents something very personal to me — a career I chose to build for myself, doing work I genuinely enjoy while continuing to grow creatively and professionally.

When I’m not getting a bride ready or touching, most of my world revolves around being a mom, spending time with my family and continuing to build the life and business I once only imagined.

I’m someone who watches the little details, loves learning and is always looking for ways to become better at what I do — whether that’s refining my artistry, staying something new or growing Bragandhi Makeovers.
