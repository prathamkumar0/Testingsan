Hi, I’m Sanpreet Kaur, a bridal hair and makeup artist based in Edmonton, Canada, and originally from Punjab, India.

My approach to beauty is simple — I want you to recognize yourself in the mirror, just more polished, confident and elevated. I specialize in soft, skin-focused glam, creating each look around your features, personal style and the way you want to feel.

Beyond the finished look, I believe your getting-ready experience should feel calm, comfortable and thoughtfully taken care of.
