BOOKING INFORMATION & POLICIES

Important details,
made simple.

To provide every client with a smooth, comfortable and stress-free experience, I’ve outlined a few important booking policies below. Thank you for understanding and respecting my time, artistry and services.

How to Book Deposit & Payment Punctuality Cancellations & Rescheduling Travel & Setup Health, Hygiene & Safety Changes to Your Booking A Final Note

How to Book

  • Fill out the enquiry form, email or message me with your event details.
  • I’ll confirm availability for your date and time.
  • Share any inspiration photos, outfit details or ideas so I can understand your vision.
  • A 40% deposit is required to secure your booking.
  • Once your deposit is received, your appointment is officially confirmed.

Deposit & Payment

  • A 40% non-refundable and non-transferable deposit is required to secure your date.
  • Your appointment is not confirmed until the deposit has been received.
  • The remaining balance and payment details will be communicated before your appointment.

Punctuality

  • Please be ready on time so we have enough time to create your look without feeling rushed.
  • If you’re running late, kindly send me a message as soon as possible.
  • A delay of more than 15 minutes without notice may result in a late fee or adjustments to the service in order to keep the day’s schedule on track.

Cancellations & Rescheduling

  • Deposits are non-refundable and non-transferable.
  • If you need to cancel or make changes to your booking, please let me know as early as possible.
  • Rescheduling is subject to my availability and may require a new deposit.
  • Genuine emergencies can happen. Please communicate as early as possible and I will do my best to work with you where reasonably possible.

Travel & Setup

  • For non-bridal group bookings, travel is available for a minimum of 3 people receiving services at the same location.
  • For bridal bookings, travel is available regardless of the number of additional clients.
  • Travel fees vary depending on the location and will be confirmed before booking.
  • Additional travel-related charges may apply where required, including parking, accommodation or early-morning travel.
  • Please provide a clean, well-lit getting-ready space with comfortable seating and enough room for setup.

Health, Hygiene & Safety

  • Professional hygiene and sanitation practices are followed throughout every appointment.
  • Please let me know in advance about any allergies, sensitivities, skin conditions or relevant medications that may affect your service.
  • If you have sensitive or reactive skin, I can adjust product choices where possible based on the information you provide.
  • Please avoid smoking in or immediately around the getting-ready area.

Changes to Your Booking

If you would like to add services, change your location, adjust your ready time or make another change after booking, please contact me as early as possible. Changes are subject to availability and additional charges may apply where applicable.

A Final Note

If you have any questions or need personalized arrangements, please don’t hesitate to reach out. My goal is to make the process from booking to getting ready feel as smooth and enjoyable as possible.
